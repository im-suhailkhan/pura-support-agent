# Troubleshooting: Connectivity Issues

This guide covers the most common Wi-Fi and app connectivity issues with Pura diffusers.

## Diffuser Won't Connect During Setup

**Step 1 — Check your network band**
Pura requires a **2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network**. Many modern routers broadcast both 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz under the same name. During setup, connect your phone to the 2.4 GHz band specifically, or temporarily disable the 5 GHz band on your router.

**Step 2 — Move closer to your router**
Keep the diffuser within 10–15 feet of your router during initial setup.

**Step 3 — Restart everything**
- Unplug the diffuser for 30 seconds, then plug it back in.
- Restart your router.
- Force-quit and reopen the Pura app.

**Step 4 — Reset the diffuser**
Hold the button on the diffuser for 10 seconds until the LED flashes red, then release. The diffuser will reboot and be ready to pair again.

## Diffuser Lost Connection After Working Fine

1. Check if your Wi-Fi password changed recently. If so, reset the diffuser (Step 4 above) and re-pair it.
2. Check if your router's firmware updated and changed settings.
3. Ensure the diffuser hasn't been moved too far from the router.

## App Shows "Device Offline"

- Open your phone's Wi-Fi settings and confirm you're on the same network as the diffuser.
- Toggle the diffuser's power off and on.
- In the Pura app, tap the device → Settings → "Reconnect Device."

## LED Blinking Patterns

| Pattern | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Blue slow pulse | Searching for Wi-Fi |
| Blue fast blink | Connecting to network |
| Red fast blink | Wrong password entered |
| Red solid | General error — reset required |
