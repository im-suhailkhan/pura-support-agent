# Pura Plus™ — Diffuser Setup

**Product:** Pura Plus™
**Topic:** Diffuser Setup

## Setup Video
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R_eGz0XNasw

## Step-by-Step Setup Instructions

1. Plug the Pura Plus into a standard wall outlet.
2. Open the Pura app on your phone.
3. Tap "Add Device" and select Pura Plus.
4. Follow the in-app steps to connect to your Wi-Fi network.
5. Insert fragrance vials into the available slots.
6. Configure your schedule and intensity settings in the app.

## Requirements
- 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network (not 5 GHz)
- Bluetooth enabled during initial pairing
- Pura app (iOS or Android)
