# Pura Car Pro™ — Cover Replacement

**Product:** Pura Car Pro™
**Topic:** Cover Replacement

## How to Replace the Cover

1. Slide the outer shell off the device by pushing at the base of the cover.
2. Align the replacement cover with the device body.
3. Slide it back on until it clicks into place.

## Important Notes
- Use only Pura-compatible covers designed for the Pura Car Pro.
- Ensure the cartridge slot opening on the cover is properly aligned before snapping the cover on.
