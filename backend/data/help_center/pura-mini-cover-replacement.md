# Pura Mini™ — Cover Replacement

**Product:** Pura Mini™
**Topic:** Cover Replacement

## How to Replace the Cover

1. Gently grip the existing cover and pull it away from the device body.
2. Align the new cover with the device, matching the mounting tabs.
3. Press firmly until the cover snaps into place.
4. Check that all airflow vents on the sides are unobstructed after installation.

## Important Notes
- Use only Pura-compatible covers designed for the Pura Mini.
- Do not force the cover — if it doesn't snap easily, re-check alignment.
- Blocked vents will reduce scent output and may cause the device to overheat.
