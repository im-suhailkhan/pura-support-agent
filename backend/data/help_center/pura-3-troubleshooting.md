# Pura 3™ — Fix a Problem

**Product:** Pura 3™
**Topic:** Troubleshooting

## App Not Detecting the Device
- Reset the device: hold the button for 10 seconds until the LED flashes.
- Make sure Bluetooth is enabled on your phone and you are within a few feet of the device.
- Ensure your Wi-Fi is 2.4 GHz.
- Force-quit the Pura app and retry "Add Device."

## No Scent Coming Out
- Check that the fragrance vial is properly inserted and not empty.
- Replace the vial if it has been in use for an extended period.
- Increase the diffusion intensity in the app.

## LED Light Issues
- Open the Pura app, go to Device Settings, and adjust light color and brightness.
- If the LED is not responding, try restarting the device by unplugging for 30 seconds.
- A factory reset (hold button 10 seconds) will restore default LED settings.
