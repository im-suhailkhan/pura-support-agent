# Pura Car™ — Features

**Product:** Pura Car™
**Topic:** Features

## Overview
The Pura Car is a plug-in car diffuser that connects to your car's 12V power port, providing app-controlled fragrance whenever you drive.

## Key Features
- Plug-in design — draws power from car's 12V port, no charging required
- App-controlled scent intensity
- Automatic start and stop tied to car power
- Compatible with Pura Car fragrance cartridges
- Compact design fits most standard power ports

## Pricing
- Device: ~$45–$55
- Fragrance cartridge refills: ~$10–$15

## Best For
- Drivers who prefer a plug-in, always-ready diffuser
- Those who don't want to manage battery charging
