# Pura Car Pro™ — FAQ's & Guides

**Product:** Pura Car Pro™
**Topic:** FAQ

## How long does the battery last?
The Pura Car Pro battery lasts approximately 15 hours per full charge.

## How long does a fragrance cartridge last?
A cartridge lasts approximately 30 days at around 1 hour of diffusion per day. Usage at higher intensity or for longer periods will reduce cartridge life.

## Does the Pura Car Pro need Wi-Fi?
No. The Pura Car Pro connects via Bluetooth only. It does not require a Wi-Fi connection.

## What is motion detection?
The Pura Car Pro has a built-in motion sensor that automatically starts diffusing when you enter the car and stops when you leave. This can be enabled or disabled in the Pura app.

## Where should I place the Pura Car Pro in my car?
Place it on a flat surface such as a cupholder, dashboard, or center console where it won't roll or obstruct the driver's view. The device is cordless so there are no cable limitations.
