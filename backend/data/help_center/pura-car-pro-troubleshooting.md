# Pura Car Pro™ — Fix a Problem

**Product:** Pura Car Pro™
**Topic:** Troubleshooting

## Device Not Detected in App
- Ensure Bluetooth is enabled on your phone.
- Stay within 5 ft of the Pura Car Pro during pairing — Bluetooth range is limited.
- Force-quit the Pura app and reopen it, then try "Add Device" again.
- Confirm the device is powered on (check battery level).

## Device Not Diffusing
- Check whether motion detection is enabled in the app — the device only diffuses when motion is detected if this setting is on.
- Verify the fragrance cartridge is properly inserted.
- Check that the cartridge is not empty (app shows estimated days remaining).
- Manually trigger diffusion from the app to test if the device responds.

## Battery Draining Quickly
- Reduce diffusion intensity in the app.
- Disable motion detection if the car is in a high-vibration environment that triggers it continuously.
- Recharge using the included cable; a full charge provides approximately 15 hours of use.
