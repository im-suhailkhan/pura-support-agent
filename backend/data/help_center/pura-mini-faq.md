# Pura Mini™ — FAQ's & Guides

**Product:** Pura Mini™
**Topic:** FAQ

## How long does a fragrance vial last?
A standard vial lasts approximately 30 days when used 6–8 hours per day at medium intensity. Higher intensity settings will reduce vial life.

## What spaces is the Pura Mini best for?
The Pura Mini is ideal for small spaces up to ~300 sq ft: bathrooms, entryways, home offices, and bedrooms.

## Can I use third-party fragrance vials?
No. The Pura Mini is only compatible with official Pura fragrance vials. Third-party vials will not fit and may damage the device.

## How do I control the Pura Mini?
The Pura Mini is controlled entirely through the Pura app. You can set schedules, adjust intensity, and switch between modes from the app.

## Does the Pura Mini work without Wi-Fi?
The Pura Mini requires Wi-Fi to connect to the Pura app. Without Wi-Fi it will run using the last saved settings, but you won't be able to make changes remotely.
