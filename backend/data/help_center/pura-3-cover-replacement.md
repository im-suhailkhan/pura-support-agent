# Pura 3™ — Cover Replacement

**Product:** Pura 3™
**Topic:** Cover Replacement

## How to Replace the Cover

1. Grip the outer casing at the sides and pull it away from the device body.
2. Align the replacement cover, matching any tabs or guides on the device.
3. Press firmly until the cover is fully seated and does not move.

## Important Notes
- Use only Pura-compatible covers designed for the Pura 3.
- Confirm the LED light diffuser panel (if part of the cover) is properly seated for even light output.
