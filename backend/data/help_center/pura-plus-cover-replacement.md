# Pura Plus™ — Cover Replacement

**Product:** Pura Plus™
**Topic:** Cover Replacement

## How to Replace the Cover

1. Grip the front plate of the Pura Plus and detach it by pulling firmly at the edges.
2. Align the replacement cover with the device body.
3. Press firmly until it clicks and sits flush with the device.
4. Ensure all screws or clips (if applicable) are secured before use.

## Important Notes
- Use only Pura-compatible covers designed for the Pura Plus.
- Ensure the cover does not restrict the device's airflow vents.
