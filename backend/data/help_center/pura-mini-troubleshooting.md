# Pura Mini™ — Fix a Problem

**Product:** Pura Mini™
**Topic:** Troubleshooting

## Not Connecting to Wi-Fi
- Ensure your phone is connected to a 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network during setup (not 5 GHz).
- Move the Pura Mini within 10 ft of your router during pairing.
- Restart your router, then unplug and replug the device.
- Reset the device by holding the button for 10 seconds and try pairing again.

## Weak or No Scent
- Increase diffusion intensity in the Pura app.
- The Pura Mini is rated for ~300 sq ft — if the room is larger, scent may be faint.
- Check that the fragrance vial is inserted correctly and is not empty.
- Replace the vial if it has been in use for more than 30 days at high intensity.

## Device Not Detected in App
- Make sure Bluetooth is enabled on your phone.
- Stay within a few feet of the device during the "Add Device" step.
- Force-quit the Pura app and reopen it, then try again.
- Ensure you are running the latest version of the Pura app.
