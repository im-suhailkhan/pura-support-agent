# Pura Plus™ — Fix a Problem

**Product:** Pura Plus™
**Topic:** Troubleshooting

## Weak or No Scent
- Move the Pura Plus to a more central location in the room.
- Increase the diffusion intensity in the Pura app.
- Confirm the fragrance vial is fully seated and not empty.
- The Pura Plus produces more airflow than smaller devices — ensure vents are not obstructed.

## Not Pairing with App
- Reset the device: hold the button for 10 seconds until the LED flashes.
- Ensure your phone is on a 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network during setup.
- Move the device within 10 ft of your router for the initial pairing.
- Force-quit the Pura app and reopen, then retry "Add Device."

## Device Shows Offline in App
- Check that your home Wi-Fi is working.
- Unplug the Pura Plus for 30 seconds, then plug it back in.
- If your Wi-Fi password changed recently, reset the device and re-pair it.
- In the app, go to Device Settings → "Reconnect Device."
