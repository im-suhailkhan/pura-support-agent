# How to Use the Pura Diffuser

Welcome to Pura! Your smart home fragrance diffuser makes it easy to enjoy premium scents on your schedule.

## Setting Up Your Diffuser

1. **Download the Pura app** from the App Store or Google Play.
2. **Plug in your diffuser** to a standard wall outlet. The LED ring will glow white to indicate it's on.
3. **Insert a fragrance vial** — twist it clockwise until it clicks into either slot (Slot A or Slot B).
4. **Open the Pura app** and tap "Add Device." Follow the in-app Wi-Fi setup instructions.

## Scheduling Fragrance

Once connected, go to **Schedule** in the app:
- Set active hours (e.g., 8 AM – 10 PM).
- Choose intensity: Low, Medium, or High.
- Switch between Slot A and Slot B fragrances on a timer.

## LED Status Codes

| Color | Meaning |
|---|---|
| White (solid) | Powered on, no Wi-Fi |
| Blue (pulsing) | Connecting to Wi-Fi |
| Green (solid) | Connected and diffusing |
| Red (solid) | Error — see Troubleshooting |

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How long does a vial last?**
A standard vial lasts 120–350 hours depending on intensity. The app shows estimated days remaining.

**Can I use any fragrance brand?**
Only Pura-compatible vials work with the diffuser. Third-party vials will not fit and may damage the device.

**My diffuser won't connect to Wi-Fi. What should I do?**
Ensure your phone is on a 2.4 GHz network (not 5 GHz) during setup. Restart your router and try again. If the issue persists, reset your diffuser by holding the button for 10 seconds.
