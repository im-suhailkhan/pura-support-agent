# Pura 3™ — FAQ's & Guides

**Product:** Pura 3™
**Topic:** FAQ

## Is the Pura 3 still supported?
Yes. Although it is no longer actively sold, the Pura 3 is fully supported by the Pura app and compatible with all current Pura fragrance vials.

## How many vials does the Pura 3 hold?
The Pura 3 holds two fragrance vials. You can schedule them to alternate automatically or run one at a time.

## What room size is the Pura 3 best for?
The Pura 3 works best in medium-sized rooms. For large or open-plan spaces, consider the Pura Plus.

## Can I still buy a Pura 3?
The Pura 3 is a legacy product and is no longer sold as new. Refurbished units may occasionally be available. All fragrance vials remain available for purchase.
