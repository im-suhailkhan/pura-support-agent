# Pura Car™ — FAQ's & Guides

**Product:** Pura Car™
**Topic:** FAQ

## How long does a fragrance cartridge last?
A cartridge lasts approximately 4 weeks at around 1 hour of use per day. Higher intensity settings will shorten cartridge life.

## Does the Pura Car need Wi-Fi?
No. The Pura Car connects via Bluetooth. It does not require Wi-Fi.

## Will it drain my car battery?
The Pura Car draws power only when the car is running (12V port active). It will not drain your battery when the car is off, provided your car's power port cuts off with the ignition.

## What is the difference between Pura Car and Pura Car Pro?
The Pura Car is a plug-in device powered by your car's 12V port. The Pura Car Pro is cordless and rechargeable with a built-in motion sensor. The Car Pro is more flexible but requires charging; the Pura Car is always ready as long as the car is on.
