## Step-by-Step Setup Instructions

![Pura Mini Setup Overview](https://placehold.co/800x500/7C3AED/white?text=Pura+Mini+Setup+Steps)
<!-- PLACEHOLDER: replace with official Pura Mini setup diagram -->

1. Download the Pura app from the App Store or Google Play.
2. Sign in or create a Pura account.
3. Plug the Pura Mini into a standard wall outlet.
4. Open the app and tap "Add Device."
5. Insert a fragrance vial into the device.
6. Follow in-app prompts to connect to Wi-Fi and adjust settings.

## Requirements
- 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network (not 5 GHz)
- Bluetooth enabled on your phone during initial pairing
- Pura app (iOS or Android)
