# Pura Plus™ — Features

**Product:** Pura Plus™
**Topic:** Features

## Overview
The Pura Plus is engineered for large or open-concept spaces, delivering strong, even scent distribution throughout bigger rooms.

## Key Features
- Powerful airflow system for faster and wider scent distribution
- Dual fragrance vial slots — alternate or blend two scents
- App control and manual controls on the device
- Designed for large rooms and open floor plans
- Compatible with all Pura fragrance vials

## Pricing
- Device: ~$79.99
- Fragrance refills: ~$12–$18 per vial

## Best For
- Living rooms
- Open-plan spaces
- Large bedrooms
- Common areas
