## Step-by-Step Setup Instructions

![Pura Car Pro Setup Overview](https://placehold.co/800x500/7C3AED/white?text=Pura+Car+Pro+Setup)
<!-- PLACEHOLDER: replace with official Pura Car Pro setup diagram -->

1. Download the Pura app from the App Store or Google Play.
2. Power on the Pura Car Pro (it is cordless and rechargeable — no outlet needed).
3. Enable Bluetooth on your phone and stay within 5 ft of the device.
4. Open the Pura app and tap "Add Device," then select Pura Car Pro.
5. Insert a Pura Car fragrance cartridge into the device.
6. The device will begin diffusing automatically when motion is detected.
