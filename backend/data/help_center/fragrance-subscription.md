# Fragrance Subscription

The Pura Subscription lets you get your favorite fragrances delivered automatically so you never run out.

## How It Works

1. Choose your fragrance(s) and delivery frequency: every 30, 60, or 90 days.
2. Subscribers receive **15% off** every order and free shipping on orders over $30.
3. Easily swap, pause, or cancel anytime from the Pura app or your account at pura.com.

## Managing Your Subscription

- **Swap a fragrance:** Go to Account → Subscriptions → tap the item → "Change Fragrance."
- **Skip a delivery:** Open your upcoming order and tap "Skip This Delivery" at least 3 days before the scheduled ship date.
- **Cancel:** Go to Account → Subscriptions → "Cancel Subscription." Cancellations take effect immediately; no partial refunds are issued for already-billed cycles.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Will I be charged before my order ships?**
Yes. Your payment method is charged 1–2 days before your scheduled ship date.

**Can I have multiple subscriptions?**
Yes. You can subscribe to multiple fragrances with independent delivery schedules.

**What if a fragrance I subscribe to is out of stock?**
We'll email you and hold your order until it's restocked, or you can choose a substitute in the app.
