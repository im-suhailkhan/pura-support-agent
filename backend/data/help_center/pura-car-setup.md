# Pura Car™ — Diffuser Setup

**Product:** Pura Car™
**Topic:** Diffuser Setup

## Setup Video
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R_eGz0XNasw

## Step-by-Step Setup Instructions

1. Plug the Pura Car into your car's power port (12V/cigarette lighter).
2. Download the Pura app and sign in or create an account.
3. Open the app and tap "Add Device," then select Pura Car.
4. Follow the in-app pairing steps.
5. Insert a Pura Car fragrance cartridge.
6. Adjust scent intensity in the app.

## Requirements
- Car with a 12V power port
- Bluetooth-enabled phone
- Pura app (iOS or Android)
