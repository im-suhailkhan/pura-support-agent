# Pura Car™ — Cover Replacement

**Product:** Pura Car™
**Topic:** Cover Replacement

## How to Replace the Cover

1. Remove the front panel by pulling gently from the edges.
2. Align the new cover with the device body.
3. Press firmly until the cover clicks and sits flush.

## Important Notes
- Use only Pura-compatible covers designed for the Pura Car.
- Ensure the cartridge slot and power port openings are aligned correctly before pressing the cover on.
