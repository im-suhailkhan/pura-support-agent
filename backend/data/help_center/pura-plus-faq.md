# Pura Plus™ — FAQ's & Guides

**Product:** Pura Plus™
**Topic:** FAQ

## What spaces is the Pura Plus best for?
The Pura Plus is designed for large rooms and open-plan layouts. It has stronger airflow than the Pura Mini or Pura 3, making it effective in spaces that smaller diffusers can't adequately cover.

## Will fragrance be used faster on the Pura Plus?
Yes. Because the Pura Plus has more powerful airflow, fragrance vials may be consumed faster compared to smaller devices, especially at high intensity settings.

## How many vials can the Pura Plus hold?
The Pura Plus has dual vial slots, so you can insert two fragrances and alternate between them or run them on a schedule.

## Can I control it without the app?
Yes. The Pura Plus has manual controls on the device itself, though the app provides the full range of scheduling and intensity options.

## Is the Pura Plus compatible with all Pura vials?
Yes. It works with all standard Pura fragrance vials.
