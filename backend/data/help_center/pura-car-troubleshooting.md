# Pura Car™ — Fix a Problem

**Product:** Pura Car™
**Topic:** Troubleshooting

## No Scent
- Check that the fragrance cartridge is fully inserted and not empty.
- Increase the diffusion intensity in the Pura app.
- Ensure the device is properly plugged into the car's power port and receiving power.

## App Connection Issues
- Make sure Bluetooth is enabled on your phone.
- Stay within a few feet of the device during pairing.
- Force-quit the Pura app and retry the connection.
- Unplug the device for 10 seconds, replug, and attempt to reconnect.

## Weak Scent
- Increase the intensity setting in the app.
- Check that the cartridge has not passed its usage life (~4 weeks at 1 hr/day).
- Ensure the device is not obstructed by objects in the cupholder or console.
