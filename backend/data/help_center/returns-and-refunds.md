# Returns and Refunds

We want you to love your Pura experience. If something isn't right, we're here to help.

## Return Policy

- **Diffusers:** Eligible for return within **30 days** of delivery if unused and in original packaging.
- **Fragrance vials:** Non-returnable once opened due to hygiene and product integrity reasons.
- **Gift sets:** Follow the same policy as individual items above.

## How to Initiate a Return

1. Email **support@pura.com** with your order number and reason for return.
2. Our team will respond within 1–2 business days with a prepaid return shipping label.
3. Pack the item securely and drop it off at any UPS location.
4. Refunds are processed within 5–7 business days of us receiving the returned item.

## Damaged or Defective Items

If your diffuser arrived damaged or stops working within the warranty period (1 year from purchase), we will send a replacement at no cost. Please email **support@pura.com** with:
- Your order number
- A brief description of the issue
- A photo of the damage (if applicable)

## Warranty Coverage

The Pura diffuser is covered by a **1-year limited warranty** against manufacturing defects. The warranty does not cover:
- Physical damage caused by dropping or misuse
- Water damage
- Use of non-Pura fragrance vials

## Frequently Asked Questions

**When will I receive my refund?**
Once we receive your return, allow 5–7 business days for the refund to appear on your original payment method.

**Can I exchange instead of return?**
Yes! Let us know in your support email and we'll arrange an exchange for the same or equivalent value.

**What if my vial leaked during shipping?**
Contact us immediately at support@pura.com with a photo. We'll send a replacement vial free of charge.
