# Pura Car Pro™ — Features

**Product:** Pura Car Pro™
**Topic:** Features

## Overview
The Pura Car Pro is a cordless, rechargeable car diffuser with smart motion detection — it starts diffusing automatically when you enter your car and stops when you leave.

## Key Features
- Cordless and rechargeable — no need to plug into a car power port
- Motion detection: automatically starts and stops diffusion when you get in and out of the car
- App control and manual intensity adjustment
- Fragrance cartridge lasts up to ~30 days (at ~1 hr/day usage)
- Battery lasts approximately 15 hours per charge
- Connects via Bluetooth (no Wi-Fi required)

## Pricing
- Device: $49.99
- Fragrance cartridge refills: ~$10–$15

## Best For
- Commuters who want a hands-free, automatic car fragrance experience
- Anyone who prefers not to use car power ports
